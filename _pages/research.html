01 / Foundation-model adaptation

Adapting representations to a new sensing domain.

The question

How can a pretrained Transformer capture a target domain’s statistics without updating every model parameter?

My contribution

I developed histogram-based parameter-efficient tuning for sonar classification. A trainable histogram module captures feature distributions alongside frozen Transformer components, providing a distribution-aware route to adaptation.

Evidence & scope

The work evaluates passive sonar recordings and active sonar imagery. On VTUAD, the reported accuracy is 91.8%, compared with 89.8% for the conventional adapter baseline. The evaluation also compares trainable parameters and feature representations. Results are specific to the evaluated datasets; the code supports inspection and further experiments.

02 / Physiological time series

Using physiology when labels are expensive.

The question

Can physiological relationships help estimate blood pressure when collecting personalized ground-truth labels is burdensome?

My contribution

I developed physics-informed neural-network models as part of a collaborative study of cuffless blood-pressure estimation. The approach combines supervised learning with physiological constraints derived through Taylor approximations.

Evidence & scope

The published study reports an average fifteenfold reduction in ground-truth training data relative to the tested time-series baselines, while retaining strong prediction performance on its evaluated bioimpedance datasets. This is a research evaluation, with performance tied to those datasets and protocols.

03 / Integrated sensing

Connecting electronics, signals, and inference.

The question

How can a wearable integrate physiological measurements and learning algorithms while operating within a limited power budget?

My contribution

During my master’s research, I designed an ECG/EDA wearable system from the circuitry and Bluetooth Low Energy electronics through to supervised stress-detection algorithms.

Evidence & scope

The project achieved 94% stress-detection accuracy in an 18-participant study and threefold battery life relative to the evaluated alternatives. Those findings reflect the study’s experimental conditions and comparison devices.

  • IEEE Sensors Journal, 2022

Current directions

Multiple representations. Limited data.

Waveform–spectrogram fusion

My dual-encoder work combines waveform and spectrogram representations through differentiable Choquet-integral fusion. It studies complementary representations while limiting the trainable parameter space.
