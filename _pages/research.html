01 / Computer vision & digital health

Vision-language models for digital pathology.

I evaluate a pretrained computational pathology vision-language model for predicting ethanol drinking status from nonhuman primate liver whole-slide images with few labeled examples, comparing visual adapters and textual task residuals with attention-based multiple instance learning. In four-shot experiments, visual adaptation achieved 78.1% mean balanced accuracy versus 43.5% for the ABMIL baseline, using subject-disjoint evaluation over 30 runs.

02 / Foundation-model adaptation

Distribution-aware adaptation for classification.

I developed and integrated a learnable histogram module into pretrained Transformers to capture target-domain feature distributions while keeping the backbone frozen. The method enables parameter-efficient adaptation for passive and active sonar classification, with comparisons of accuracy, trainable parameters, and feature representations. On VTUAD, it achieved 91.8% accuracy versus 89.8% for the conventional adapter baseline.

  • IGARSS 2026 paper

03 / Physiological time series

Physics-informed models for physiological prediction.

I co-developed physics-informed neural networks for cuffless blood-pressure estimation from physiological time series. The approach incorporates physiological relationships expressed through Taylor approximations into supervised training. The collaborative study reported an average 15 times reduction in ground truth training labels compared with the tested time-series baselines.

  • npj Digital Medicine, 2023

04 / Biomedical sensing

An integrated wearable for stress detection.

During my master’s research, I designed an ECG/EDA wearable stress-detection system, from its circuitry and Bluetooth Low Energy electronics to supervised learning algorithms. The 18-participant study achieved 94% classification accuracy and threefold battery life relative to the comparison devices.

  • IEEE Sensors Journal, 2022
